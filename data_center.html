The Renewable Energy Supply Chain for Data Centers: Opportunities, Challenges, and Future Directions

This research delves into the renewable energy supply chain for data centers, addressing the sector's rising energy consumption driven by AI, cloud computing, and digital services. It examines opportunities for integrating solar, wind, and geothermal energy while highlighting challenges such as intermittency, land use, and storage limitations. The study explores innovations like battery energy storage systems and power purchase agreements, emphasizing their role in enhancing sustainability and reducing the carbon footprint of data center operations.
